How to play DicePoker

DicePoker is a mix of strategy and chance. You and the AI roll 5 dice to create the best possible poker hand to win.

Game Setup

  • Click on "Start Game" to begin a new game
  • Each player rolls 5 dice at the start of the game
  • The AI's hand is shown at the top and your hand is shown at the bottom

Reroll Phase

  • You can reroll ONCE all, some, or none of the dice
  • Select the dice you'd like to reroll using the checkbox (leave it blank for to reroll none)
  • Click on "Reroll" to continue after deciding how many dice to reroll
  • The AI will also choose and reroll dice based on its own strategy

Result

  • After rerolling, the hands are evaluated based on the ranking below
(BEST ROLLS AT TOP OF THE TABLE)
